Scope: `web/app`, `web/components` (landing `/`, `/pay`, `/merchant`, header, footer), crayon MS Paint redesign.

Priority = rule tier: Hard Gate HIGH, Purpose-Gate MEDIUM, Quality Lock LOW.

## HIGH (Hard Gate)

1. **R-02 em dash.** `app/layout.tsx:10` page title "Mezher — pay with your Bitcoin…" shows in the browser tab and search results. Fix: "Mezher: pay with your Bitcoin, keep your Bitcoin".
2. **R-36 / R-38 inaccurate claim "no gas".** Hero lead "No selling, no gas." and the meta description "No gas." are untrue for balance-only payments, which the payer now sends with their own gas (`BalanceOnlyNeedsPayer`). Fix: "no gas when you borrow" or drop the claim from the hero.
3. **R-17 unsourced number.** Final CTA "Pay something in 30 seconds." has no measurement behind it. Fix: remove the time, e.g. "Got a Trove? Pay for a test coffee."
4. **R-19 + WCAG 2.2.2 endless motion.** The demo mockup loops forever (7 s timeline) and the hero star sways forever (`landing.module.css:160`). Auto-moving content longer than 5 s needs a way to pause, and an endless loop with no trigger is a named slop pattern. Fix: mockup plays when it scrolls into view, runs 2 loops then rests on the final frame, plus a small "Replay" button; star stops after its first sway.
5. **R-23 logo created without confirmation.** The crayon BTC coin logo (header, footer) and the "M" coin favicon (`app/icon.svg`) were drawn by Claude without the owner asking for a logo. Fix: owner confirms them as the logo, or they are replaced by the word "Mezher" until a logo is chosen.
6. **R-03 mobile not verified.** Nothing checked at 390 px. Known risks from code: nav links are 4 px padded (tap target under 44 px), wallet button min-height 42 px (under 44 px), header row (logo + 2 links + wallet button) may overflow. Fix: 44 px targets, check and fix every page at 390 px.
7. **R-35 no recorded click-through.** Only screenshots were taken. Missing: Try the Demo, I'm a merchant, nav, footer links, FAQ toggles, wallet button, paste-a-link (valid, invalid, Enter key), Create payment link, Copy link, Replay, keyboard Tab order. Fix: run it and record each element with its result.

## MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)

8. **R-04 Lucide icons in a hand-drawn UI.** `StatusLine`, `Field`, `PrimaryButton`, `PaymentsTable`, `merchant/page.tsx` use Lucide's thin rounded strokes (ExternalLink, Copy, Check, AlertTriangle, Loader2), the one machine-perfect element next to crayon drawings. Fix: replace with crayon doodles from `Doodle.tsx` (check, warning, spinner scribble, link out).
9. **R-12 shadow on everything.** Every card, button, input frame, hero sketch, phone and laptop carries the same hard offset shadow, so nothing reads as raised. Fix: keep the shadow on the primary button, the hero sum and the mockup devices; cards and inputs sit flat.
10. **R-01 / Part 3 orange accent spread everywhere.** Orange is on the primary buttons, logo, active nav circle, "Keep" circle, every section underline, the 12 MUSD box and the borrow hatch. Fix: orange only for the Pay action and the "Keep" circle; section underlines and nav circle in ink or yellow.
11. **C-3 / R-05 the same story told twice.** "Watch a coffee get paid" (mockup) and "How it works" (3 cards) both narrate link, sign, paid, back to back. Fix: fold the three steps into the mockup as captions synced to the animation and drop the separate 3-card section, or keep the cards and cut the mockup.
12. **R-19 fade-up on every section.** Every landing block uses the same reveal (fade + lift), the "template animation" pattern. The owner asked for smooth scroll motion, so this is direction (R-37 asks to name it and confirm). Fix if dropped: reveal only the mockup and the final CTA; other sections appear normally.
13. **R-04 star doodle.** "Star" is a named generic glyph; used on the 12 MUSD box and the receipt as a "done" sticker. Fix: keep it only on the receipt moment (real success) with the reason written, remove it from the hero.

## LOW (Quality Locks)

14. **R-06 uppercase tracked labels.** `.section-label` ("PAYMENTS", "YOUR BITCOIN CREDIT") and footer headings ("USE IT", "ON-CHAIN") are uppercase with letter-spacing. Fix: sentence case on the yellow highlighter.
15. **R-31 / Part 3 decisions not written in the design doc.** `docs/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` still describes the old dark receipt design; the crayon tokens, dials and one-line reasons (paper ruling = sketchbook, offset shadow = sticker, Shantell Sans = owner's "comic not Comic Sans") live only in the spec. Fix: rewrite DESIGN_SYSTEM.md to the crayon system with the dials.
16. **R-29 palette breadth.** Ink/paper plus orange, yellow, green, red and blue. Green and red are status colours (fine); blue appears as decoration in illustrations and the focus ring. Fix: blue only for links and focus, illustrations use orange/yellow/green.
17. **Demo without legal pages (UI skill).** The checkout asks for a payment but there is no Terms or Privacy page. Testnet only and labelled, so low. Fix: a short "Testnet terms" page linked in the footer, or leave as is for the hackathon.
